
# SupportPilot Sample Support FAQ

## Login Problems

If a customer cannot log in to their account:

1. Ask the customer to verify that they are using the correct registered email address.
2. If they have forgotten their password, direct them to the password reset option.
3. If resetting the password does not resolve the issue, collect the error message and escalate the issue to technical support.
4. Never ask the customer to share their password.

## Duplicate Billing

If a customer reports being charged twice for the same subscription:

1. Ask the customer for the dates of both transactions and their payment reference numbers.
2. Check whether both transactions were completed successfully.
3. Do not promise or confirm a refund before the billing team reviews the transactions.
4. Escalate suspected duplicate charges to the billing support team for investigation.
5. Keep the customer informed about the investigation and follow the company's refund policy.

## Refund Requests

If a customer requests a refund:

1. Explain that refund eligibility depends on the applicable product policy and transaction details.
2. Ask for the order or transaction reference.
3. Forward the request to the billing team for review.
4. Do not guarantee refund approval before the billing team makes a decision.

## API 403 Errors

If a customer reports an API 403 Forbidden error:

1. Ask them to verify that their API key is valid and has the required permissions and access scope.
2. Ask which endpoint returned the error.
3. If the issue continues, collect the timestamp and request ID, if available.
4. Never ask the customer to share secret API keys publicly.
5. Escalate unresolved issues to technical support.

## App Crashes

If the application crashes:

1. Ask the customer to update the application and restart it.
2. If the problem continues, collect the device model, operating system version, app version, and steps that reproduce the crash.
3. Escalate repeated crashes to technical support.

## Human Review and Safety

1. Do not invent company policies or unsupported troubleshooting steps.
2. Do not promise refunds without approval from the billing team.
3. Do not claim an issue is fixed without evidence.
4. All AI-generated replies must be reviewed and explicitly approved by a human support agent before being sent.
